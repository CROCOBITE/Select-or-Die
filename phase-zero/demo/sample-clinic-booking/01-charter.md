## 1. Purpose
Let patients book, change and cancel outpatient appointments online without calling the clinic.

## 2. The problem today
Patients wait on hold to book; no-show rates are high because reminders are manual.

## 3. What will be different – objectives
| ID | Objective | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| OBJ-01 | Online share of bookings | Online bookings ÷ all bookings | ≥ 40% after 3 months |
| OBJ-02 | No-show rate | Missed ÷ booked appointments | Baseline, then −10% |

## 4. Scope
**In scope**
- Web and mobile booking
- Reminders by SMS
- Cancellation and rescheduling

**Out of scope**
- Payment
- Tele-consultation
